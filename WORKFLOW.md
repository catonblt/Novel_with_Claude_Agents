# Novel Writing Workflow Guide

A detailed guide to the collaborative process of writing literary fiction with the 8-agent system.

## Overview

The workflow is designed to be iterative, flexible, and human-centered. While phases are presented sequentially, in practice you'll move fluidly between them, returning to earlier phases as understanding deepens and the work evolves.

## Phase 1: Vision & Conceptualization

### Goals
- Articulate core vision for the novel
- Identify central themes and questions
- Establish tone, style, and aesthetic goals
- Determine scope and ambition

### Primary Agent: Story Advocate

### Process

1. **Initial Vision Discussion**
   - Story Advocate facilitates conversation
   - You share: inspirations, themes, character seeds, setting ideas, emotional goals
   - Discussion questions:
     - What kind of novel is this? (tone, style, genre positioning)
     - What themes obsess you?
     - What reading experience do you want to create?
     - What's the emotional heart of the story?

2. **Vision Documentation**
   - Story Advocate synthesizes discussion
   - Creates vision document capturing:
     - Core themes
     - Tonal and stylistic goals
     - Central dramatic questions
     - Aesthetic aspirations
     - Comp titles and influences

3. **Team Briefing**
   - Story Advocate shares vision with all agents
   - Each agent considers implications for their domain
   - Agents ask clarifying questions

### Outputs
- Vision document
- Thematic framework
- Tonal guidelines
- Aesthetic references

---

## Phase 2: Structural Planning

### Goals
- Design overall narrative architecture
- Map character arcs
- Plan thematic development
- Create chapter-level framework

### Primary Agent: Architect

### Process

1. **Story Exploration**
   - Architect proposes multiple structural approaches
   - Options might include:
     - Different act structures
     - Linear vs. non-linear chronology
     - Single vs. multiple POV
     - Frame narratives or other forms
   - Each option presented with trade-offs

2. **Collaborative Selection**
   - Story Advocate presents options
   - You discuss preferences and concerns
   - Team refines chosen approach
   - Architect develops detailed framework

3. **Character Arc Mapping**
   - Character Psychologist develops major characters
   - Architect maps arcs against structure
   - Integration with plot and theme
   - Identification of key transformation moments

4. **Detailed Outlining**
   - Chapter-by-chapter breakdown
   - Scene sequencing
   - Thematic beats placement
   - Pacing rhythm mapping

### Supporting Agents
- **Character Psychologist**: Character development
- **Research Agent**: Historical/cultural context if needed
- **Continuity Editor**: Timeline establishment

### Outputs
- Master structure document
- Chapter-by-chapter outline
- Character arc documents
- Thematic architecture map
- Timeline framework

---

## Phase 3: Deep Development

### Goals
- Flesh out characters fully
- Research necessary details
- Design settings comprehensively
- Establish story bible

### Primary Agents: Character Psychologist, Research Agent, Atmosphere Agent, Continuity Editor

### Process

1. **Character Deep Dive**
   - Character Psychologist creates comprehensive dossiers
   - Psychology, background, voice, relationships
   - Sample interior monologues
   - Dialogue voice samples
   - Story Advocate presents for feedback

2. **Research and World-Building**
   - Research Agent investigates all factual elements:
     - Historical period (if applicable)
     - Professional domains
     - Geographic locations
     - Cultural practices
   - Creates research briefings and fact sheets

3. **Setting and Atmosphere Design**
   - Atmosphere Agent develops all major locations
   - Sensory palettes for different settings
   - Atmospheric moods planned
   - Integration with themes

4. **Story Bible Creation**
   - Continuity Editor establishes tracking systems:
     - Character details database
     - Timeline (chronological and narrative)
     - Location inventory
     - Relationship matrix
     - Plot thread tracker
     - Knowledge management system

### Outputs
- Character dossiers (all major characters)
- Research briefings
- Location guides
- Atmospheric frameworks
- Comprehensive story bible

---

## Phase 4: Scene Writing

### Goals
- Draft scenes with high literary quality
- Integrate all elements seamlessly
- Maintain consistency
- Create emotionally resonant, thematically rich prose

### Primary Agent: Prose Stylist

### Process

1. **Scene-by-Scene Development**
   - Working from outline, develop scenes in sequence (or key scenes first)
   - For each scene:
     - Architect provides scene purpose and beats
     - Character Psychologist provides character states and voices
     - Atmosphere Agent provides sensory/atmospheric guidance
     - Prose Stylist drafts the scene
     - Research Agent verifies factual elements
     - Continuity Editor tracks all details

2. **Collaborative Drafting**
   - Prose Stylist creates initial draft
   - Integrates inputs from all agents:
     - Character voice (Character Psychologist)
     - Sensory detail (Atmosphere Agent)
     - Structural purpose (Architect)
     - Factual accuracy (Research Agent)

3. **Scene Review**
   - Story Advocate presents draft
   - You provide feedback
   - Team refines based on direction
   - Continuity Editor updates story bible

### Iteration Pattern per Scene
1. Architect: Scene purpose and structure
2. Character Psychologist: Character voices and psychology
3. Atmosphere Agent: Setting and sensory detail
4. Prose Stylist: Draft integration
5. Research Agent: Fact-check
6. Continuity Editor: Detail tracking
7. Story Advocate: Present to you
8. Refine based on feedback

### Outputs
- Drafted scenes
- Updated story bible
- Scene-by-scene feedback integration

---

## Phase 5: Chapter Assembly & Review

### Goals
- Assemble scenes into chapters
- Ensure chapter-level coherence
- Check pacing and rhythm
- Maintain consistency

### Primary Agents: Architect, Beta Reader

### Process

1. **Chapter Assembly**
   - Architect ensures scenes flow into coherent chapters
   - Prose Stylist ensures transitions work
   - Atmosphere Agent ensures atmospheric consistency
   - Character Psychologist ensures character arc progression

2. **Chapter-Level Review**
   - Beta Reader reads each chapter fresh
   - Assesses:
     - Engagement and pacing
     - Emotional resonance
     - Clarity and comprehension
     - Character believability
     - Prose quality
   - Provides detailed feedback

3. **Revision Based on Beta Feedback**
   - Story Advocate synthesizes Beta Reader feedback
   - Identifies patterns and priorities
   - Proposes revisions
   - Team implements approved changes

4. **Continuity Check**
   - Continuity Editor verifies consistency within chapter
   - Checks against story bible
   - Flags any contradictions
   - Manages any necessary updates

### Outputs
- Complete chapters
- Beta reader reports
- Revision tracking
- Updated story bible

---

## Phase 6: Manuscript-Level Revision

### Goals
- Ensure overall coherence
- Strengthen thematic development
- Perfect pacing across whole work
- Polish prose throughout
- Verify complete consistency

### Primary Agents: All agents collaborating

### Process

1. **Complete Manuscript Beta Read**
   - Beta Reader reads entire manuscript
   - Holistic assessment:
     - Overall pacing and structure
     - Character arc completion
     - Thematic coherence
     - Emotional satisfaction
     - Ending effectiveness
   - Comparative analysis (positioning vs. comp titles)

2. **Structural Revision**
   - Architect assesses overall structure based on beta feedback
   - Identifies any structural weaknesses
   - Proposes macro-level revisions
   - You approve direction

3. **Comprehensive Continuity Audit**
   - Continuity Editor full manuscript check
   - Verifies all timelines
   - Catches any remaining contradictions
   - Ensures all plot threads resolved (or intentionally left open)

4. **Prose Polish**
   - Prose Stylist refined pass on language
   - Sentence-level improvements
   - Rhythm and music enhancement
   - Voice consistency verification

5. **Final Fact-Check**
   - Research Agent comprehensive verification
   - All factual claims checked
   - Historical accuracy confirmed
   - Cultural representation reviewed

### Outputs
- Revision plan
- Polished manuscript
- Final continuity report
- Final fact-check report

---

## Phase 7: Final Polish & Completion

### Goals
- Achieve publishable quality
- Ensure every element serves the whole
- Complete all documentation
- Prepare for submission/publication

### Primary Agents: Prose Stylist, Continuity Editor, Story Advocate

### Process

1. **Line-by-Line Polish**
   - Prose Stylist final pass
   - Every sentence refined
   - Rhythm perfected
   - Language beautiful and precise

2. **Final Consistency Pass**
   - Continuity Editor final verification
   - Story bible completeness check
   - Any last-minute details tracked

3. **Final Beta Read**
   - Beta Reader reads polished manuscript
   - Confirms improvements
   - Identifies any remaining issues
   - Provides final assessment

4. **Completion Documentation**
   - Story Advocate creates final project summary
   - Documents all key decisions
   - Compiles research sources
   - Organizes all project materials

### Outputs
- Polished, complete manuscript
- Final story bible
- Complete research documentation
- Project archive

## Ongoing Practices Throughout

### Communication Protocols

**Story Advocate as Hub**
- All proposals to you come through Story Advocate
- All feedback from you goes through Story Advocate
- Story Advocate translates between perspectives

**Regular Check-ins**
- After each major section
- When significant decisions needed
- If any agent has concerns
- If you have questions or new directions

### Flexibility Principles

**Iterate Freely**
- Return to earlier phases as needed
- Structure evolves with understanding
- Characters may surprise you—follow them
- Better solutions emerge through dialogue

**Embrace Discovery**
- Writing reveals things planning can't anticipate
- Adjust outline when character truth demands it
- Follow compelling tangents if they serve the whole
- Trust the collaborative process

### Decision-Making

**You Have Final Authority**
- Creative vision is yours
- Agents provide expertise and options
- You make all final decisions
- Team implements your choices

**Trust Agent Expertise**
- Agents argue for quality and plausibility
- Take their concerns seriously
- But you can overrule if vision demands it
- Make them work to earn implausible choices

## Common Workflow Patterns

### Pattern A: Scene-by-Scene
Best for: Structured writers, complex plots, tight timelines
- Develop detailed outline first
- Write scenes in sequence
- Strong upfront planning
- Systematic execution

### Pattern B: Key Scenes First
Best for: Discovering through writing, character-driven stories
- Outline major structural points
- Write key emotional/dramatic scenes
- Fill in connective tissue
- Discover through doing

### Pattern C: Iterative Spiraling
Best for: Complex literary fiction, thematic exploration
- Initial rough structure
- Draft loosely
- Revise and refine in waves
- Structure solidifies through revision

### Pattern D: Discovery Writing with Agent Support
Best for: Experimental, intuitive writers
- Minimal upfront planning
- Agents track and organize as you write
- Structure emerges organically
- Heavy revision to create coherence

## Tips for Effective Collaboration

1. **Communicate Clearly**: Tell Story Advocate what you want, what concerns you, what excites you

2. **Ask Questions**: If you don't understand a proposal, ask for clarification

3. **Push Back**: If something doesn't feel right, say so—even if you can't articulate why yet

4. **Trust the Process**: The iterative back-and-forth creates quality

5. **Stay Flexible**: Better ideas emerge; be willing to adjust

6. **Maintain Vision**: Hold onto what matters most while being flexible on how to achieve it

7. **Use Agent Expertise**: They know craft; leverage that knowledge

8. **Make Final Calls**: Don't get paralyzed by options—decide and move forward

9. **Celebrate Progress**: Acknowledge good work and milestones

10. **Remember**: This is your book, supported by expert collaborators
